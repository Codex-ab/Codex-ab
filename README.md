### 🚀 Featured Project — CardsBay

> **A production-grade, multi-region e-commerce platform built end-to-end.**

CardsBay is a full-stack online store that handles everything a modern commerce product needs — from a
CMS-driven storefront to a data-rich admin dashboard.

- 🌍 **Multi-region & multi-currency** — availability filtering by region with server-side FX conversion
- 💳 **Real checkout** — Stripe + crypto payments, transactional order flow & order snapshots
- 🧮 **Dynamic pricing engine** — hero/offer discounts applied consistently across every surface
- 🖥️ **CMS-driven content** — editable homepage, promotions, banners, footer & About pages
- 📊 **Admin dashboard** — store analytics, live alerts feed, and a store-overview command center
- 📧 **Notifications & email** — preference-aware customer emails and newsletter broadcasting

**Tech stack:** `Next.js (App Router)` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `TailwindCSS` · `TanStack Query` · `Zustand` · `NestJS` · `MongoDB` · `Zod` · `Stripe`
